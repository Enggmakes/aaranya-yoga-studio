# IRA's yoga house

A serene, editorial-quality web application for **IRA's yoga house** — a boutique sanctuary for mindfulness, hatha yoga, breathwork, and restorative practice.

## Design Philosophy

Crafted following intentional anti-slop design principles:

- **Earthy, Restrained Palette**: Calming forest green, warm ivory/sand, and muted olive.
- **Editorial Typography**: Elegant serif headlines (`Cormorant Garamond`) paired with clear, readable body text (`DM Sans`).
- **Grounded Aesthetics**: Generous whitespace, natural photography, clean responsive navigation, zero artificial SaaS fluff or distracting neon animations.
- **Mobile-First Experience**: High-touch mobile drawer with seamless scroll locking, backdrop dismiss, and quick contact actions.

## Pages & Structure

- **Home (`/`)**: Hero section, "Why Choose IRA's Yoga House" pillars, popular programs, journey CTA, and student testimonials.
- **About (`/about`)**: Studio philosophy, mindful teaching approach, instructors, and sanctuary highlights.
- **Programs (`/programs`)**: Comprehensive view of all 6 classes (Hatha, Ashtanga, Meditation, Pranayama, Beginner, Personalized Sessions) with detailed modals and booking actions.
- **Testimonials (`/testimonials`)**: Student reflections and experiences from the mat.
- **Contact (`/contact`)**: Validated booking enquiry form, studio hours, address, phone, email, and WhatsApp connection.
